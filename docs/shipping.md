# Shipping

Zones: `domestic` (DE), `eu`, `world` (everything else). Rate = base + per-kg surcharge for every kg after the first.
Free shipping: domestic above 50 EUR, EU above 100 EUR, never for world.
Carriers: the cheapest carrier serving the zone and weight wins. Delivery estimate skips weekends; orders after 14:00 UTC ship the next business day.
